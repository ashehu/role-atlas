How fresh are these roles?

Verified means the employer's public hiring system or job page confirmed the opening within the last 14 days. Newly sourced does not mean verified. AI, machine learning, data science, and forward-deployed roles are included in the AI focus.

A missing role is flagged first, then archived after a second definitive check on a later day. Blocked pages and timeouts need verification. Archived roles remain in the index with your notes; use the freshness filter to see them.
